## Anthropic — Research Brief for Resume Writer  
**Target role:** Manager of Applied AI Architecture, Partnerships

### Company priorities
- Anthropic positions itself as an **AI safety and research company** focused on building AI systems that are:
  - **Reliable**
  - **Interpretable**
  - **Steerable**
- Its mission emphasizes ensuring AI is **safe and beneficial** for users and society.
- Anthropic is a **public benefit corporation** headquartered in San Francisco.
- Its flagship product family is **Claude**, a series of large language models.

### Partner ecosystem and market motion
The role is explicitly designed to accelerate adoption of Anthropic products through a partner ecosystem that includes:

- **Global and Regional System Integrators (GSIs/RSIs)**
  - Examples named in the job description: Accenture, Deloitte, TCS, Infosys.
- **Cloud providers**
  - AWS, Google Cloud Platform, and Microsoft Azure.
- **Strategic technology partners**
- Partner-led implementation and pre-sales for:
  - Claude for Enterprise
  - Claude Code
  - Anthropic API

A visible example is Anthropic’s **multi-year partnership with Accenture**. Anthropic frames the partnership around a shared commitment to **responsible AI**, combining:
- Anthropic’s **Constitutional AI principles**
- Accenture’s **AI governance expertise**

The stated enterprise outcome is enabling AI use with **confidence, transparency, and accountability**.

### Role-relevant signals
This is a senior people-management and technical-GTM leadership role, not solely an individual contributor architecture position.

Key mandate areas:
- Build, hire, manage, mentor, and develop a **Partner Solutions Architect** team.
- Establish repeatable processes and best practices for **partner-led pre-sales**.
- Serve as the senior technical counterpart to GTM Partnerships leadership.
- Co-develop partner strategy and align Sales, Partnerships, Product, and Engineering.
- Enable GSI and cloud-partner technical organizations and AI practices.
- Lead joint solution development for high-value, industry-specific generative-AI applications.
- Create or codify:
  - Reference architectures
  - Deployment best practices
  - Partner enablement approaches
- Personally support strategic, partner-led customer deals.
- Turn deployment feedback into actionable input for Product and Engineering roadmaps.
- Represent Anthropic in technical partner forums, including workshops, cloud summits, hackathons, developer communities, and thought-leadership settings.

### Candidate profile implied by the role
The resume should make credible evidence of the following especially easy to find:

1. **Leadership at scale**
   - 3+ years managing pre-sales or partner-facing technical teams.
   - Hiring, performance management, coaching, and career development.
   - Building foundational teams amid ambiguity and rapid growth.
   - Ideally, leadership of partner-facing SA organizations during hypergrowth.

2. **GSI and cloud-partner experience**
   - Building or scaling relationships and technical motions with GSIs, consultancies, or AWS/GCP/Azure.
   - Understanding indirect revenue, joint GTM, partner enablement, and partner-led delivery.
   - Helping consultancies establish or scale AI/ML practices, such as enablement, certification, or joint-solution programs.

3. **Enterprise generative-AI technical depth**
   - LLM architecture
   - Prompt engineering
   - Evaluation
   - API integrations
   - Production enterprise deployments
   - Industry-specific GenAI solutions

4. **Executive technical partnership**
   - Trusted engagement with C-suite leaders, partner leadership, and engineering teams.
   - Ability to translate technical architecture into partner strategy, joint solutions, and customer outcomes.

5. **Responsible-AI orientation**
   - Directly connect AI deployment work to safe, responsible, governed, transparent, and accountable use.
   - Anthropic’s external partnership messaging indicates that responsible AI and governance are central commercial as well as technical themes.

### Resume-writing guidance
Prioritize quantified examples that show a candidate:
- Built or led a partner-facing solutions architecture/pre-sales team.
- Expanded partner capability or seller/delivery readiness across GSIs or cloud partners.
- Created reusable AI architectures, solution accelerators, enablement frameworks, or technical certification pathways.
- Influenced pipeline, indirect revenue, deal velocity, production deployment, or partner-sourced/partner-led outcomes.
- Led complex enterprise AI implementations through APIs, evaluation, architecture, and deployment.
- Embedded safety, governance, transparency, or accountability into customer and partner AI programs.
- Converted customer/partner field feedback into product, engineering, or roadmap decisions.
