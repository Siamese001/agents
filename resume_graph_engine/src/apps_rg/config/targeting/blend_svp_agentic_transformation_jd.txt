SVP, Agentic Transformation
Blend (Blend360) | Columbia, MD (Onsite / Hybrid)

About Blend (Blend360)
Blend is a premier AI services provider and data, AI, and marketing consulting firm that partners with ambitious Fortune 1000 and Private Equity organizations to unlock transformational value through the power of data science, AI, technology, and people. Founded in 2016, Columbia, MD. 1001-5000 employees. Website: https://blend360.com/

About the Role
As Senior Vice President of Agentic Transformation, you will lead senior-most, non-technical consulting relationships with Fortune 1000 and Private Equity clients, guiding them through complex agentic transformation engagements, operating model redesign, and autonomous enterprise AI adoption.

Responsibilities
- Own senior-most consulting relationships with C-Suite executives (CEO, CFO, COO, CAIO, CTO) at Fortune 1000 and PE-portfolio clients across transformation engagements.
- Serve as Blend360's trusted strategic advisor to client leadership—guiding them through the business case, organizational implications, and risk landscape of agentic adoption.
- Lead executive steering committees, board-level briefings, and transformation governance forums on behalf of our clients.
- Cultivate deep, multi-year relationships that expand Blend360's footprint within accounts and generate new opportunities through referral and reputation.
- Lead the design and facilitation of Agentic Transformation roadmaps—defining the sequencing, business value, and organizational change required to move clients from current state to agentic maturity.
- Frame transformation in terms of business outcomes: cost efficiency, revenue growth, competitive differentiation, risk reduction—not technology outputs.
- Draw on deep institutional knowledge of prior technology transformation waves (ERP, cloud migration, digital, data/analytics) to contextualize what is different and what is the same about the agentic era.
- Partner with Blend360's technical and delivery teams to ensure the business and organizational elements of transformation are integrated seamlessly into the overall program design and execution.
- Assess and guide organizational readiness for agentic transformation—spanning culture, workforce, governance, operating model, and change capacity.
- Help clients design the structures, roles, and accountability models needed to sustain agentic operating models beyond initial implementation.
- Drive stakeholder alignment and manage organizational resistance at senior levels, including navigating political dynamics within complex enterprises.
- Translate technical complexity into clear, resonant narratives for non-technical executives and boards.
- Collaborate with Blend360's leadership team to evolve our Agentic Transformation service portfolio, methodologies, and go-to-market narrative.
- Contribute to thought leadership—white papers, keynotes, executive roundtables—that positions Blend360 as the definitive partner for enterprise agentic transformation.
- Support business development and proposal efforts, including executive-level presentations and solution framing for new client opportunities.
- Mentor and develop consulting talent, codifying your hard-won transformation expertise into Blend360's institutional playbooks and frameworks.

Skills & Qualifications
- Partner Level Experience with top tier management consulting firm (McKinsey, Bain, BCG, Alix Partners, PwC, etc.).
- 20+ years of progressive consulting or senior advisory experience, with a significant portion spent leading large-scale technology transformation engagements at Fortune 1000 or equivalent organizations.
- Proven track record of owning and growing C-Suite relationships in an advisory or consulting capacity—not just participating in them.
- Deep, hands-on experience with at least two or more of the major enterprise transformation waves: ERP (SAP, Oracle), cloud migration, digital transformation, data/analytics modernization, or platform consolidation.
- Demonstrable experience in Private Equity environments—either as an advisor to PE-portfolio companies during transformation programs or in an operating role within a PE-backed business.
- Familiarity with the emerging agentic AI landscape—including enterprise AI agents, autonomous process orchestration, and the organizational implications of human-agent collaboration—sufficient to engage credibly with technically sophisticated clients and internal teams.
- Commanding gravitas and credibility with CEOs, board members, and investor groups—particularly in ambiguous, high-stakes environments. Executive presence.
- The ability to translate technical complexity and organizational ambiguity into clear, compelling strategic narratives that drive executive alignment and decision-making. Strategic framing.
- A track record of building multi-year, trust-based relationships with senior clients that transcend individual engagements. Relationship depth.
- Experience guiding organizations through significant resistance, uncertainty, and disruption—with the empathy and tenacity to see transformations through. Change leadership.
- The ability to anchor all recommendations in financial impact and business value—not technology for its own sake. Business acumen.
- Comfort working in a matrix consulting environment, partnering with technical leads, delivery managers, and subject matter experts to drive integrated client outcomes. Collaborative execution.
