About the Role

The Global Head of Agentic AI Transformation will lead the enterprise-wide strategy, development, and implementation of agentic AI solutions and associated end-to-end process re-engineering across AIG's businesses and functions globally. This executive will be accountable for partnering in transforming underwriting, claims, operations, finance, and corporate functions by combining advanced autonomous AI agents, modern workflow design, and rigorous process-improvement disciplines such as Lean and Six Sigma.

This role will report to the Global Chief Data Officer and will partner with executive leadership to define the company's agentic AI vision, drive platform and architecture decisions, design and scale enterprise-wide AI-powered operating models, and ensure that all deployments deliver measurable improvements in productivity, accuracy, service levels, and cost efficiency.
