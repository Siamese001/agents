## Anthropic — Resume Writer Research Brief  
**Target role:** Manager of Applied AI Architecture, Partnerships

### Company priorities
- Anthropic positions itself as an **AI safety and research company** focused on building AI systems that are:
  - **Reliable**
  - **Interpretable**
  - **Steerable**
- Its stated mission emphasizes creating AI that is **safe and beneficial** for users and society.
- The company operates as an American **public benefit corporation** and is headquartered in **San Francisco**.  
- For this role, safety is not merely a research theme: the job description frames maintaining **“best-in-class safety standards”** as part of partner-led deployment and go-to-market execution.

### Partner ecosystem signals
Anthropic’s partner motion spans three core channels named in the job description:

1. **Global and Regional System Integrators (GSIs / SIs)**
   - The role explicitly names firms such as **Accenture, Deloitte, TCS, and Infosys** as relevant experience.
   - Anthropic and Accenture announced a **multi-year partnership** in December 2025.
   - Accenture describes the partnership as supporting enterprise AI adoption through **Claude experts, certified talent, and lower-risk deployment**.
   - This suggests Anthropic values scalable partner enablement, partner certification or capability-building, and implementation support—not simply referral relationships.

2. **Cloud providers**
   - The role names **AWS, Google Cloud Platform, and Azure** as core cloud partners.
   - The manager will help deploy Anthropic products through cloud-partner technical teams and collaborate on partner programs, events, workshops, summits, and hackathons.

3. **Strategic technology partners**
   - Anthropic announced strategic partnerships involving **Microsoft and NVIDIA** in November 2025.
   - This reinforces the importance of working across major technology ecosystems and aligning technical, commercial, and product stakeholders.

### Role-relevant signals
The role is a people-leadership and ecosystem-architecture position, rather than an individual-contributor solutions architect role.

Key themes to foreground in candidate experience:

- **Built and led partner-facing technical teams**
  - Hiring, managing, mentoring, setting goals, conducting reviews, and developing both senior and junior talent.
  - Particularly relevant: experience leading teams during high growth or in ambiguous, foundational environments.

- **Scaled GSI and cloud partner motions**
  - Experience with GSIs, consultancies, AWS, GCP, Azure, or equivalent partner ecosystems.
  - Demonstrated understanding of **partner-led selling and delivery**, indirect revenue, co-selling, enablement at scale, and joint go-to-market motions.

- **Enabled enterprise AI delivery**
  - The technical profile sought includes enterprise deployment experience involving:
    - LLM architecture
    - Prompt engineering
    - Evaluation
    - API integrations
    - Production AI use cases
  - Strong evidence would include turning early AI capabilities into reusable delivery approaches, governance patterns, reference architectures, or implementation playbooks.

- **Developed joint solutions**
  - The role will lead partners to identify high-value, industry-specific generative AI use cases.
  - Relevant examples: co-creating vertical solutions, solution accelerators, reusable reference architectures, technical best practices, or deployment frameworks that improved time to deployment.

- **Owned technical pre-sales for partner-led deals**
  - The manager is expected to own the technical component of partner-led pre-sales engagements and personally support strategic deals.
  - Resume evidence should show solution architecture leadership in complex enterprise pursuits, especially where a partner served as the primary delivery vehicle.

- **Created product-feedback loops**
  - Anthropic expects the leader to gather validated field feedback from partner deployments and relay it to Product and Engineering.
  - Relevant accomplishments include converting customer or partner insights into roadmap input, product improvements, integration enhancements, or technical documentation.

- **Represented the company externally**
  - Partner workshops, cloud summits, hackathons, developer communities, technical content, and thought leadership are expressly in scope.
  - Strong signals include executive-facing technical communication, developer advocacy, partner training, speaking, or authored technical material.

### Positioning guidance
A strong resume narrative for this role should present the candidate as a leader who can connect:

**partner ecosystem strategy → technical enablement → reusable enterprise AI solutions → strategic deal execution → product feedback**

The strongest accomplishment bullets will quantify, where available:
- Team growth, hiring, retention, or promotion outcomes
- Number or tier of enabled partners
- Certifications, training completion, workshops, or practitioner reach
- Joint pipeline, indirect revenue, influenced bookings, or deal size
- Adoption, deployment volume, solution reuse, or reduced implementation time
- Number of reference architectures, accelerators, vertical solutions, or technical programs launched
- Product roadmap changes or adoption improvements driven by field feedback

### Source-based company context to reflect
Anthropic’s public language is centered on advancing AI that is **reliable, interpretable, steerable, safe, and beneficial**. For this partnerships architecture leadership role, the most aligned candidate messaging will pair commercial and ecosystem outcomes with credible attention to **responsible deployment and safety standards**.
