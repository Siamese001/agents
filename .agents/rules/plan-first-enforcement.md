# Plan-First Enforcement (`agents` Overrides)

Inherits `/plan` interactive **Proceed** (`RequestFeedback: true`), `/learn` Path A/B firewall, and root-`main` plan bans from `~/.gemini/config/plugins/core-governance/rules/plan-first-enforcement.md`.

## Direct Root-Cause Execution vs. `/plan` Mode

- **Direct Execution (`INV-REFACTOR-EXTENT-UNBOUNDED`)**: `implement`, `fix`, `/boost`, `/swarm`, `/learn`, `refactor`, `commit`, and `promote` authorize immediate end-to-end root-cause fixes across all connected producers, contracts, consumers, validators, and tests with zero file-count or blast-radius caps (`INV-WAVE-PLAN-MAX-WORDS = 0`).
- **Repository Plans**: Tracked plans live in `plans/<name>-<6hex>.md` per [.agents/rules/wave-progression.md](wave-progression.md) and [docs/templates/wave_plan_template.md](../../docs/templates/wave_plan_template.md), validated by `tools/validate_implementation_plan.py` (`config/governance/linter_profile.json`).

## Holistic Pipeline Spine Alignment (`INV-HOLISTIC-PIPELINE-PLANNING`)

When modifying intermediate pipeline lanes across `resume_graph_engine`, `outreach_engine`, or `apps_research`, inspect full-ripple dependencies across: (1) Stage-1 retrieval/ranking, (2) Stage-2 C0 allocation & `FactAnchor` schema projection, (3) Stage-3 section generation/prompt assembly, and (4) Stage-4 X2 cross-section validation. Focused single-module bug-fix plans pass `tools/validate_implementation_plan.py` without artificial bundling blocks.
